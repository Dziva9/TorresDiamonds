Questions worth asking before you buy.

Plain-language answers to the things that actually affect how a diamond or jewelry piece looks, wears and holds value to you.

Yes. Lab-grown diamonds have the same crystal structure and optical properties as mined diamonds. The difference is origin: one forms underground, the other in a controlled laboratory environment.

IGI independently grades a diamond’s characteristics such as carat, colour and clarity. A grading report gives you a third-party reference for the stone you are buying.

For many pieces, an eye-clean VS stone offers an excellent balance of appearance and value. VVS grades reduce inclusions further but may not look visibly different without magnification.

It depends on the stone and your priorities. VVS can be desirable for higher-spec pieces, but cut quality and proportions often have a larger impact on visible brilliance.

Higher karat gold contains more pure gold. 10K is harder and lower-cost, 14K balances durability and gold content, while 18K has a richer gold content and colour.

Pricing varies significantly with cut, colour, clarity, certification, setting design and metal. Compare full specifications rather than carat weight alone.

A hidden halo places small accent diamonds beneath the centre stone, adding sparkle from the side profile while keeping the face-up look cleaner.

White gold is lighter and usually less expensive. Platinum is denser, naturally white and often chosen for a heavier, premium feel.

Ordering & aftercare

Know the details before ordering.

Before committing to a piece, confirm the selected metal and size, diamond specifications, included grading documentation, total price, production and delivery estimate, and applicable return, resizing and warranty terms. Custom options and timing depend on the piece.
