A few helpful answers

Before you choose.

Are lab-grown diamonds real diamonds?

Yes. Lab-grown diamonds have the same crystal structure and fundamental physical and optical properties as natural diamonds. They are grown in a controlled environment rather than formed underground. They are different from diamond simulants such as cubic zirconia and moissanite.

What should I compare when choosing diamond jewelry?

Start with the design and how you plan to wear it. Then compare diamond cut, colour, clarity, carat weight, dimensions, metal purity and setting construction. Check whether the listed carat weight describes a centre stone, a pair of earrings or all diamonds in the piece.

What does an IGI grading report tell me?

An International Gemological Institute report records the characteristics of the diamond or jewelry described in that report. Check the report type and the details it covers, and match them to the listing. Ask which documentation is included with your chosen piece before ordering.

What is the difference between solid gold and gold-plated jewelry?

Solid gold jewelry uses a gold alloy throughout the gold component. Gold-plated jewelry has a layer of gold over another material. Gold karat describes purity: 10K is about 41.7% gold, 14K about 58.3%, and 18K 75%. The right option depends on your preferred colour, budget and the design.

Can you help me choose within my budget?

Yes. Start with your budget, the occasion and any styles you like. Torres Diamonds can help you compare options and understand the differences in stones, settings and metals. You do not need to know every diamond grade before asking for help.

Can I start a custom piece with a reference photo?

A photo, sketch or description is a useful starting point. We can explore a design around your preferences and budget. Feasibility, specifications, price and timing need to be confirmed before you approve an order; a reference image is inspiration rather than a promise of an exact reproduction.
