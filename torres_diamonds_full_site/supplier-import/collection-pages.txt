L378:

L380: ## How the catalogue is organised
L381: 
L382:   * 【6†Rings】. 75 women's rings, 59 【2†engagement rings】, 22 men's rings. Solitaire, three stone, toi et moi, halo, eternity, wedding band.

L387: ## How to choose a category
L388: 
L389: Start with the occasion, not the carat. Engagement and wedding sit in rings. A first serious gift lands on studs or a pendant. A milestone lands on a tennis bracelet or a tennis necklace, where total carat weight does the work.
L390: 
L391: Then set the band. 65 pieces sit under 2,000 CAD in the 【11†entry collection】. Above that the catalogue moves into larger centre stones and multi row pieces.
L392: Then read the grading. White diamonds are IGI certified E to F colour, VS2 and better. Fancy colour stones, including pink, vivid blue, canary yellow and cognac, are graded on the fancy colour scale instead. Designs pairing diamonds with black onyx, ruby or sapphire name those stones in the title. One merchandise item, the Draco cap, is not jewellery.
